How we worked this out

  • Nifty 50: the index's closing value at the end of each year. Dividends aren't included, so a real Nifty fund would have done a little better.
  • Gold: approximate price of 24K gold per 10g around the end of each year. Making charges, GST and storage aren't included.
  • Fixed Deposit: an approximate SBI 1-year FD rate, renewed every year and compounded quarterly, before tax.
  • After inflation: shows the value in money from the year you started, using India's yearly CPI inflation (World Bank).
  • All figures are approximate and for learning only. Past returns don't predict future returns. This is not investment advice.
