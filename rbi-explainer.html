🧭 How an RBI decision reaches your wallet

🏛️RBI changes the repo rateThe rate at which it lends to banks
→
🏦Banks' costs changeBorrowing money gets cheaper or costlier for them
→
💳Loan & FD rates moveEMIs and deposit rates follow, usually over a few months
→
🛒Spending & prices adjustCheaper loans → more spending; costlier loans → prices cool

In short: rates down → cheaper loans, lower FD returns, more spending. Rates up → costlier loans, better FD returns, slower price rises.
